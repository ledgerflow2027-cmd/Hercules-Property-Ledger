❔ Help & support

Quick guidance for the current preview

🏠 Properties, tenants & vendors

Add property, tenant and vendor details from their respective workspaces. Uploaded identity documents are not securely stored in this prototype.

🧾 Receipts & payments

Receipt and payment vouchers are locally saved source records. They do not automatically create posted accounting entries or confirm money received or paid.

📚 Accounting reports

General Ledger, Trial Balance and financial statements are based on posted journals only. Draft vouchers and unposted journals are excluded.
